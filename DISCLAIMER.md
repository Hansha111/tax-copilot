# Disclaimer

**This project is not tax, legal or accounting advice.**

- It is a portfolio / learning project.
- It runs **only on synthetic data**. Every GSTIN it produces is randomly generated, passes the checksum format only, and is tied to vendors and clients whose names start with `DEMO`. No real taxpayer, client or business data is used or stored.
- Reconciliation results, explanations and any drafted text (for example, DRC-01C Part B reasons) are illustrations. They must be reviewed by a qualified Chartered Accountant before anyone relies on them.
- The software never files, uploads or writes anything to the GST portal, Tally or any other system.
- GST rules, thresholds and portal behaviour change. Always check the current official sources (CBIC, GST Council, the GST portal).
